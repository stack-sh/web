## IBM Plex fonts

This application bundles the following unmodified Latin WOFF2 font files:

- IBM Plex Sans Variable, packaged by `@fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans` 5.3.0
- IBM Plex Mono, packaged by `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono` 5.3.0

IBM Plex is copyright IBM Corp. and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full license text is available in [`licenses/OFL-1.1.txt`](./licenses/OFL-1.1.txt).
